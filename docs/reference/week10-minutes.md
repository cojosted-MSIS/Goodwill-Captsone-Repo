# Week 10 stakeholder meeting summary

Source: meeting summary email written by Collin Stedman.

## Meeting Summary

Today's meeting focused on reviewing the updated prototype, our initial implementation timeline, and the AI Architecture deliverable.

We demonstrated the updated prototype, which can now support both new-neighbor entries and recurring case-note updates. Because we do not have access to the actual HMIS database, the prototype continues to use synthetic data and a mock HMIS environment.

Dennis confirmed that signature-required documents can remain outside our current project scope and be documented as a future production consideration. We also discussed the need for additional verification when multiple neighbors have similar identifying information, such as the same name and date of birth.

Hannah helped clarify that case notes and service documentation should be considered the core MVP functions. Service documentation is especially important because it records outreach activities used for grant and funding reporting.

We also walked through the initial implementation timeline. The current dates are intentionally conceptual, and the attached question sets will help us better understand realistic development timing, Goodwill approvals and governance, and Horizon House training/change-management requirements before we finalize the timeline.

Finally, JD reviewed our AI Architecture document, including the proposed role of AI, ownership responsibilities, costs, security considerations, and the distinction between our current prototype and a future production solution.

## Key Decisions / Alignment

- Keep signature-required documents outside the current prototype scope.
- Prioritize case notes and services as the primary MVP functions.
- Continue using synthetic data and a mock HMIS for the prototype.
- Add stronger record verification for neighbors with similar identifying information.
- Refine the implementation timeline after receiving feedback from Horizon House, Goodwill, and DeveloperTown.
- Continue researching broader HMIS querying/search capabilities as a potential future enhancement.
- Gather stakeholder feedback on the AI Architecture before finalizing the recommendation.

## Priorities & Action Items

- Goodwill: Review and respond to the attached Goodwill timeline questions.
- DeveloperTown: Review and respond to the attached DeveloperTown timeline questions.
- Goodwill / Horizon House: Review the attached AI Architecture document and share any feedback, questions, or concerns.

## Where We Left Off

The prototype is continuing to mature, and we now have a clearer definition of the core MVP around case notes and service documentation. Our immediate focus is validating the assumptions behind the implementation roadmap and AI architecture so our final recommendation reflects realistic development, governance, change-management, and technical requirements.
